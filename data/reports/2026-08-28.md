### 15:00 - Prelim Benchmark Payrolls Revision

**Benchmark Payrolls Revision** - Annual re-benchmarking of the payroll survey against actual tax records, which can rewrite a whole year of job growth in one line.

- _Published by:_ Bureau of Labor Statistics, preliminary in late summer, final in February
- _Why gold cares:_ A large downward revision means the labour market was weaker than the Fed thought all along, which is dovish and gold-positive.
- _What to watch:_ The size of the revision against the prior year's figure. Six figures is a serious number.
- _Catch:_ Once-a-year event that most calendars underrate. It can reprice the whole rate path in a single print.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $15-40

### 15:00 - Revised UoM Inflation Expectations

**UoM Consumer Sentiment** - Michigan's household survey, released in preliminary and revised form at 15:00 UK.

- _Published by:_ University of Michigan, monthly
- _Why gold cares:_ The sentiment number is minor. The inflation-expectations component attached to it is not.
- _What to watch:_ The 1-year and 5-10 year inflation expectations. The Fed watches the long-run figure closely, so a jump there is a genuine rate signal.
- _Catch:_ Small sample, frequently revised, and prone to reflecting petrol prices more than anything else.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $3-12
