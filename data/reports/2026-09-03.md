## Event briefings

### 13:30 - Unemployment Claims

**Initial Jobless Claims** - New unemployment benefit filings in the prior week. Released every Thursday at 13:30 UK.

- _Published by:_ Department of Labor, weekly
- _Why gold cares:_ The highest-frequency labour signal available. Individually minor, but a trend of rising claims shifts the whole rate narrative.
- _What to watch:_ The four-week moving average rather than the weekly print, and continuing claims for whether people are finding new work.
- _Catch:_ Distorted by holidays, weather and state-level administrative quirks. One bad week means nothing.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $3-10

### 15:00 - ISM Services PMI

**ISM Services PMI** - The services-sector equivalent, covering the bulk of the US economy. Released 15:00 UK.

- _Published by:_ Institute for Supply Management, third business day of the month
- _Why gold cares:_ More economically meaningful than the manufacturing survey and consequently a bigger mover when it surprises.
- _What to watch:_ Prices paid, as a services-inflation signal, and employment as an NFP hint.
- _Catch:_ Noisier month to month than its manufacturing counterpart.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $10-25
