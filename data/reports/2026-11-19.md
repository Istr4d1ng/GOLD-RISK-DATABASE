**US / Israel – Iran** is the live one, steady on 5 headlines in the last 48 hours. The one flashpoint that hits gold through every channel at once. Safe-haven bid on the headline, Treasuries bid so nominal and real yields fall, and - uniquely - a credible threat to the Strait of Hormuz spikes crude, which lifts inflation expectations. _Working against it:_ The dollar is also a haven, so risk-off bids the dollar too and cancels part of gold's gain. This is why some escalation days produce a far smaller gold move than the headlines imply.

_How long this usually lasts:_ Days. Gold spikes on the exchange of fire and gives most of it back within a week unless the oil disruption is real and sustained, because only then does it reach the Fed's reaction function.
