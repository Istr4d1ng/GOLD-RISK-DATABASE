## Event briefings

### 15:00 - ISM Manufacturing PMI

**ISM Manufacturing PMI** - Survey of purchasing managers. Above 50 means expansion. Released 15:00 UK.

- _Published by:_ Institute for Supply Management, first business day of the month
- _Why gold cares:_ A timely growth signal that arrives before the hard data. Weakness feeds recession pricing, which is usually gold-supportive.
- _What to watch:_ The prices-paid and new-orders sub-indices carry more information than the headline.
- _Catch:_ Manufacturing is a small share of US output, so the survey overstates its own importance.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $8-20

### 15:00 - JOLTS Job Openings

**JOLTS Job Openings** - Job openings, hires and quits. Released 15:00 UK, roughly six weeks in arrears.

- _Published by:_ Bureau of Labor Statistics, monthly
- _Why gold cares:_ The vacancy-to-unemployed ratio is a labour-tightness gauge the Fed has cited directly.
- _What to watch:_ The quits rate, as a measure of worker confidence.
- _Catch:_ Very stale by release, and the response rate has been falling for years.
- _Rough prior expectation:_ $3-12
