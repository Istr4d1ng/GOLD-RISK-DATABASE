15:00 Benchmark Payrolls Revision — Prelim Benchmark Payrolls Revision

Annual re-benchmarking of the payroll survey against actual tax records, which can rewrite a whole year of job growth in one line.

Published by
Bureau of Labor Statistics, preliminary in late summer, final in February
Why gold cares
A large downward revision means the labour market was weaker than the Fed thought all along, which is dovish and gold-positive.
What to watch
The size of the revision against the prior year's figure. Six figures is a serious number.
The catch
Once-a-year event that most calendars underrate. It can reprice the whole rate path in a single print.
Rough prior expectation
$15-40
