escalating US / Israel – Iran — 8 headlines, weight 10
Why gold cares
The one flashpoint that hits gold through every channel at once. Safe-haven bid on the headline, Treasuries bid so nominal and real yields fall, and - uniquely - a credible threat to the Strait of Hormuz spikes crude, which lifts inflation expectations. Roughly a fifth of seaborne oil passes through that strait.
Working against it
The dollar is also a haven, so risk-off bids the dollar too and cancels part of gold's gain. This is why some escalation days produce a far smaller gold move than the headlines imply.
How long it lasts
Days. Gold spikes on the exchange of fire and gives most of it back within a week unless the oil disruption is real and sustained, because only then does it reach the Fed's reaction function.
What would escalate it
Direct state-on-state strikes rather than proxies; any interference with shipping in Hormuz; attacks on oil infrastructure; withdrawal from nuclear monitoring.

escalating Trade and tariffs — 4 headlines, weight 5
Why gold cares
Indirect but real. Tariffs are an inflation impulse and a growth drag at once, which is the hardest combination for the Fed and therefore raises uncertainty about the rate path.
Working against it
Also dollar-positive in the short run, which works against gold. The net effect is genuinely ambiguous and often small.
How long it lasts
Weeks, when it changes the inflation outlook. Announcement headlines alone rarely stick.
What would escalate it
Rates above expectations, retaliation, or extension to critical minerals and energy.

active Russia / Ukraine / NATO — 4 headlines, weight 8
Why gold cares
Two channels: haven demand, and the structural one - sanctions on Russian reserves in 2022 taught central banks that dollar reserves can be frozen, which is a large part of why official-sector gold buying has been so persistent since.
Working against it
Day-to-day headlines are largely priced. The structural bid shows up in central bank demand data, not in a day's price action.
How long it lasts
Headlines: days. The reserve-diversification effect: years, and it is arguably the single biggest reason gold has re-rated.
What would escalate it
Any Article 5 discussion, strikes on NATO territory, nuclear rhetoric or doctrine change, or new sanctions touching reserves or energy.
