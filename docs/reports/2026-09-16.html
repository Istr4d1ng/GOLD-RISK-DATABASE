19:00 FOMC Rate Decision

Published together with FOMC Statement — one release, one reaction.

The Federal Open Market Committee sets the target range for the federal funds rate. Announced at 19:00 UK (14:00 ET), followed 30 minutes later by the Chair's press conference.

Why gold cares
Gold pays no interest, so its opportunity cost is the real yield on cash and Treasuries. Anything that lowers expected real rates raises gold, and vice versa. This is the single most direct transmission channel there is.
What to watch
The decision itself is usually priced in. The move comes from three other things: changes to the statement's language, the dot plot on projection meetings, and the tone of the press conference - which frequently reverses the initial statement reaction.
The catch
Two-leg event. Gold often moves one way on the 19:00 statement and the opposite way during the 19:30 presser. Judging the day on the first ten minutes is how people get caught.
Rough prior
$20-60 on the day, more on projection meetings

No measured history yet — the figures above are a prior, not a statistic.

19:00 Summary of Economic Projections (dot plot)

Every committee member's forecast for growth, unemployment, inflation and the appropriate policy rate. Published four times a year alongside the rate decision.

Why gold cares
The dot plot repriced the whole forward rate curve in one release. Gold trades the curve, not the current rate, so a shifted median dot moves it harder than the decision.
What to watch
The median dot for the current and next year, and how many members moved. The longer-run dot matters for the real-rate anchor.
The catch
The dots are not a commitment and the Chair usually says so in the presser, which can unwind the initial move.

$30-70 on the day
