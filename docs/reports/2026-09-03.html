Event briefings — what each one is and what it does to gold

13:30 Initial Jobless Claims — Unemployment Claims

New unemployment benefit filings in the prior week. Released every Thursday at 13:30 UK.

Published by
Department of Labor, weekly
Why gold cares
The highest-frequency labour signal available. Individually minor, but a trend of rising claims shifts the whole rate narrative.
What to watch
The four-week moving average rather than the weekly print, and continuing claims for whether people are finding new work.
The catch
Distorted by holidays, weather and state-level administrative quirks. One bad week means nothing.
Rough prior expectation
$3-10

15:00 ISM Services PMI — ISM Services PMI

The services-sector equivalent, covering the bulk of the US economy. Released 15:00 UK.

Published by

Why gold cares
More economically meaningful than the manufacturing survey and consequently a bigger mover when it surprises.
