Headline and core consumer inflation, month on month and year on year. Released 13:30 UK (08:30 ET).

Why gold cares
The most reliable single driver of Fed expectations, and therefore of gold. A hot print lifts real yields and the dollar and knocks gold; a cool one does the reverse.
What to watch
Core m/m is what the market trades - headline is noisier because of energy. A 0.1pp surprise against forecast is enough to move the metal.
The catch
Gold's first move is sometimes reversed within the hour if the internals (shelter, supercore) contradict the headline.
