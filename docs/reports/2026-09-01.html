Event briefings — what each one is and what it does to gold

15:00 ISM Manufacturing PMI — ISM Manufacturing PMI

Survey of purchasing managers. Above 50 means expansion. Released 15:00 UK.

Published by
Institute for Supply Management, first business day of the month
Why gold cares
A timely growth signal that arrives before the hard data. Weakness feeds recession pricing, which is usually gold-supportive.
What to watch
The prices-paid and new-orders sub-indices carry more information than the headline.
The catch
Manufacturing is a small share of US output, so the survey overstates its own importance.
Rough prior expectation
$8-20
