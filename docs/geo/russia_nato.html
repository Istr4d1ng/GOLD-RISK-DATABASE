How it reaches gold

Sanctions→Reserve diversification→Central bank buying→Gold
Why gold cares
Two channels: haven demand, and the structural one - sanctions on Russian reserves in 2022 taught central banks that dollar reserves can be frozen, which is a large part of why official-sector gold buying has been so persistent since.
Working against it
Day-to-day headlines are largely priced. The structural bid shows up in central bank demand data, not in a day's price action.
How long it lasts
Headlines: days. The reserve-diversification effect: years, and it is arguably the single biggest reason gold has re-rated.
What would escalate it
Any Article 5 discussion, strikes on NATO territory, nuclear rhetoric or doctrine change, or new sanctions touching reserves or energy.
